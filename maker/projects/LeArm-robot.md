# LeArm 六轴机械臂



LeArm is an intelligent robotic arm aiming at education. In order to meet the interest of robotic arm from the majority of robot enthusiasts, we launched the high performance desktop robotic arm. It has 6 servos which allow it to grab in any direction. The robotic arm also supports the users to program on the computer and mobile phone APP.
