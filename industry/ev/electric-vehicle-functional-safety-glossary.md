# 汽车功能安全名词解释



## 英文缩写

- **ASIL**：Automotive Safety Integrity Level，汽车安全完整性等级
- **QM**：Quality Management，质量管理
- **PHA**：Preliminary Hazard Analysis，预期危害分析
- **SG**：Safety Goal，安全目标
- **DIA**：Development Interface Agreement，开发接口协议
- **FTTI**：Fault Tolerant Time Interval，故障容裕时间间隔
- **FDT**：Fault Dection Time，故障检测时间
- **FRT**：Fault Reaction Time，故障处理时间
- **FSC**：Functional Safety Concept，功能安全概念
- **FSR**：Functional Safety Requirements，功能安全要求
- **TSC**：Technical Safety Concept，技术安全概念
- **TSR**：Technical Safety Requirements，技术安全要求
- **HSR**：Hardware Safety Requirements，硬件安全要求
- **SSR**：Software Safety Requirements，软件安全要求
- **NFR**：Non-Functional requirement，非功能要求
- **SM**：Safety Mechanisms，安全机制
- **FTA**：Fault Tree Analysis，故障树分析
- **DFA**：Dependent Failure Analysis，相关性失效分析
- **FMEDA**：Failure Mode Effect & Diagnostic Analysis，失效模式影响 & 诊断分析
- **FMEA**：Failure Mode Effect Analysis，失效模式影响分析
- **STS**：Safety Test Strategy，安全测试策略
- **STP**：Safety Test Plan，安全测试计划
- **STR**：Safety Test Report，安全测试报告
- **V&V**：Verification & Validation，验证与确认
- **SaC**：Safety Case，安全档案
- **CCF**：Common Cause Failure，共因失效
- **FFI**：Freedom from interference，免干扰设计
- **PMHF**：Probabilistic Metric for random Hardware Failures，随机硬件失效率
- **SPFM**：Single-Point Fault Metric，单点失效度量
- **LFM**：Latent-Fault Metric，潜在失效度量
- **E/E system**： Electrical and/or Electronic system，电子电器系统



## 术语定义

- Item：system or array of systems or a function on vehicle level to which ISO26262 is applied.
- System：Combination of components interacting to carry out a defined functional purpose.
- Component：Independent physical element sold as a product to the customer
- Verification：Determination of completeness and correct specification or implementation of requirements from a previous phase.
- Validation：Assurances，based on examination and test，that the safety goals are adequate and have been achieved with a sufficient level in integrity.
- Verification Review：Verification activity to ensure that the result of a development activity fulfils the project requirement, or technical requirements, or bolt.
- Confirmation review：Confirmation that a work product (3.185) provides sufficient and convincing evidence of their contribution to the achievement of function safety  considering the corresponding objectives and requirement of ISO26262
- Safety Audit：Examination of an implemented process with regard to the process objectives
- Safety Assessment：Examination of whether a characteristic of an item or element achieves the ISO 26262
